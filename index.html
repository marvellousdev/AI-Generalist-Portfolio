02 / METHODOLOGY

A repeatable evaluation rubric

I use the same dimensions across response reviews, with a transparent 1–5 scoring scale.

01
◎

Accuracy

Are statements and calculations correct based on available evidence?

02
◇

Instruction fit

Does the answer respect the user’s requested format and constraints?

03
↗

Reasoning

Is the conclusion supported by valid, consistent logic?

04
☰

Clarity

Can a reader understand the answer without unnecessary confusion?

Scoring guide: 1 = poor, 2 = weak, 3 = acceptable with issues, 4 = good, 5 = excellent. Scores are qualitative human judgments—not model-generated truth claims.
